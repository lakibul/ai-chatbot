# NodiDesk — User Guide for Support Agents

## Creating a ticket
Go to Tickets > New Ticket. Enter the customer email, subject, and description, then choose a priority (P1, P2 or P3).
Tickets created from incoming emails are created automatically with priority P3.

## Priorities and response targets (SLA)
| Priority | Meaning | First response target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Service down or data loss | 1 hour |
| P2 | Major feature not working | 4 hours |
| P3 | Question or minor issue | 1 business day |

## Assigning and reassigning
Tickets are assigned round-robin to available agents in the team. To reassign, open the ticket and choose Assign > select agent. Only team leads can reassign P1 tickets.

## Macros
Macros are saved replies. Create them under Settings > Macros. A macro can also change the ticket status and priority.

## Auto-close
If a ticket is waiting for the customer and the customer does not reply for 7 days, the ticket is closed automatically. The customer can reopen it within 30 days by replying.

## Attachments
The maximum attachment size is 25 MB per file.

## Languages
The agent interface is available in English and Bangla. Change it under Profile > Language.
