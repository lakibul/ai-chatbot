# Nodi Tech Ltd. — Leave Policy

Version 2.1 · Effective 1 January 2024
STATUS: SUPERSEDED. This document was replaced by the Employee Handbook v3.0, effective 1 January 2026. Kept for record only.

## Annual leave
Employees receive 15 days of annual leave per year. Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward with no expiry date.

## Casual and sick leave
Casual leave: 10 days. Sick leave: 14 days.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely 1 day per week with team lead approval.
