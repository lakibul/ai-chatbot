# Nodi Tech Ltd. — IT Security Policy

Version 1.4 · Effective 1 February 2026 · Owner: IT Security Team

## 1. Accounts and passwords
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
- Passwords do not need to be changed on a schedule. Change your password immediately if you suspect it has been exposed.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts, using an authenticator app. SMS codes are not accepted.
- Never share your password, including with IT staff. IT will never ask for it.

## 2. Devices
- All laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled (BitLocker on Windows, FileVault on macOS).
- Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- USB storage devices are blocked by default. To request an exception, raise a ticket of type SEC-EXC in the IT portal.

## 3. Network
- The company VPN, NodiSecure, is required whenever you use public Wi-Fi.
- NodiSecure is always required to access production servers, from any network.

## 4. Reporting incidents
- Report any security incident (lost device, suspicious login, malware) to security@noditech.example within 1 hour of noticing it.
- Report phishing emails using the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook. Do not forward phishing emails to colleagues.

## 5. Violations
Breaking this policy may lead to disciplinary action, including termination.
