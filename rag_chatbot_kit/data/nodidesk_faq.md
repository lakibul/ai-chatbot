# NodiDesk — Customer FAQ

**What are your support hours?**
Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM Bangladesh time. Enterprise customers get 24/7 support for P1 issues.

**Where is my data stored?**
Customer data is hosted in our Dhaka data centre, with encrypted backups in Singapore.

**Can I try NodiDesk for free?**
Yes. Every plan has a 14-day free trial, and no card is required.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are available within 7 days of your first payment. Email billing@noditech.example.

**Can I use NodiDesk in Bangla?**
Yes. Since release 2.6, the agent interface is available in Bangla.

**Do you support single sign-on (SSO)?**
SSO is available on the Enterprise plan only.
