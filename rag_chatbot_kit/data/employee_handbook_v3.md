# Nodi Tech Ltd. — Employee Handbook

Version 3.0 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: Human Resources
This handbook replaces all earlier HR policy documents, including the Leave Policy v2.1 (2024).

## 1. Working hours
Standard office hours are 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM, Sunday to Thursday. Friday and Saturday are weekly holidays.
Core hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM. All employees must be available (in office or online) during core hours.

## 2. Probation and confirmation
New employees serve a probation period of 3 months. Confirmation is decided by the team lead and HR at the end of probation.

## 3. Leave
| Leave type | Days per year | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Annual leave | 18 | Apply at least 3 working days in advance |
| Casual leave | 10 | Can be applied on the same day |
| Sick leave | 14 | More than 2 consecutive days needs a medical certificate |
| Maternity leave | 6 months (paid) | Apply at least 8 weeks before the expected date |
| Paternity leave | 10 working days | Must be taken within 3 months of the birth |

### Carry forward
Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March, otherwise they lapse. Casual and sick leave cannot be carried forward.

### How to apply
All leave is applied through the NodiHR portal. Your team lead approves annual and casual leave; HR approves maternity and paternity leave.

## 4. Remote work
After completing probation, employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week.
Remote days must be requested in NodiHR and approved by the team lead by 5:00 PM on the previous working day.
Employees on probation cannot work remotely except with written approval from the department head.
Remote employees receive an internet allowance (see the Expense Reimbursement Policy).

## 5. Notice period
Employees on probation must give 30 days' notice. Confirmed employees must give 60 days' notice.
