# NodiDesk — Plans and Pricing

NodiDesk is Nodi Tech's customer support helpdesk software. Prices are per agent per month, in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT), excluding VAT.

| Feature | Starter | Growth | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price per agent / month | BDT 1,200 | BDT 2,500 | Custom quote |
| Maximum agents | 5 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Channels | Email | Email + live chat | Email + live chat + phone |
| Uptime SLA | None | 99.5% | 99.9% |
| API rate limit | Not available | 60 requests/min | 600 requests/min |
| Single sign-on (SSO) | No | No | Yes |
| Dedicated account manager | No | No | Yes |

## Billing
- Annual billing gets a 15% discount compared with monthly billing.
- Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. No card is needed for the trial.
- Refunds are available within 7 days of the first payment.

## Upgrades and cancellation
- You can upgrade at any time; the price difference is charged pro rata.
- You can cancel at any time. After cancellation, your data can be exported for 30 days, then it is permanently deleted.
